Confirmations workflow

Stop losing booked production to no-shows.

A booked visit is not yet production. When a patient does not show, or cancels late and no one fills the slot, the chair sits empty and the production is already gone. Praxora treats confirmations as a workflow: confirm early, catch cancellations early, and backfill the opening before it is lost.

Where the leak happens

  • A visit is confirmed once, then never checked again — and the patient simply does not arrive.
  • A cancellation comes in, but no one acts on it the same day, so the slot stays empty.
  • The front desk only learns about a no-show when the patient is already late, and there is no one ready to take the slot.
  • Confirmations are counted as messages sent, not as visits actually kept.

By the time the chair is empty, the leak happened days earlier — at confirmation, at cancellation, or at backfill.

How Praxora works for confirmations

  1. Confirm with a purpose Each upcoming visit gets a confirmation step with a clear status — confirmed, unconfirmed, or needs a person — instead of a single message sent to everyone.
  2. Catch cancellations the day they happen A cancellation becomes a case immediately: the open slot is visible, and the backfill list is already ranked, so the front desk is not starting from scratch.
  3. Record what actually happened Kept, rescheduled, cancelled, or no-show — the outcome is recorded for every visit. That record is what lets the practice see which visits, and which patients, need a different approach next time.

That is the whole workflow: a case is captured, owned, worked to a recorded outcome, and only then closed. See also dental patient follow-up software and dental missed call recovery.

Frequently asked questions

How many times should a visit be confirmed?

There is no universal standard. What matters is that an unconfirmed visit stays visible and has a next action, rather than being assumed confirmed because a message was sent.

What should happen when a patient cancels?

The slot should enter the openings workflow the same day, with a ranked list of patients who could take it. A cancellation that waits until tomorrow is usually a lost slot.

Is this the same as appointment reminders?

No. Reminders tell a patient when to arrive. A confirmation workflow tracks whether the visit is actually kept, what happens when it is not, and whether the slot gets filled.
