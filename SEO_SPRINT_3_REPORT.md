# Praxora SEO Sprint 3 — Competitor Interception Report

## Strategy

Sprint 3 captures software-comparison and alternative-search intent without writing attack pages.

The editorial pattern is:

Competitor/category search  
→ describe the current product accurately  
→ identify the workflow the practice is actually trying to solve  
→ define neutral comparison criteria  
→ introduce Follow-Up Recovery when the problem is unresolved next action / ownership / outcome  
→ send high-intent readers to the Calculator or Free Leak Review

## New Interception Pages

### MaxAssist Alternatives for Dental Follow-Up Prioritization

Primary intent:
`MaxAssist alternatives`

Comparison dimensions:

- Opportunity source
- Current patient state
- Why follow-up stopped
- Previous relevant touch
- Priority
- Next action
- Owner
- Human review
- Outcome

The page explicitly acknowledges MaxAssist's current breadth and its public prioritization positioning.

### RecallMax Alternatives for Dental Follow-Up

Primary intent:
`RecallMax alternatives`

The page clearly explains that RecallMax is now MaxAssist, then separates recall-specific, unscheduled-treatment, and broader follow-up buying needs.

### Weave vs Follow-Up Recovery

Primary intent:
`Weave vs follow up recovery`

The page accurately frames Weave as a broad communication platform with phones, texting, missed-call texts, reminders, analytics, and AI receptionist capabilities.

The category distinction is:
communication infrastructure vs focused unresolved-opportunity recovery.

### Dental Intelligence vs Follow-Up Recovery

Primary intent:
`Dental Intelligence vs follow up recovery`

The page explicitly states that Dental Intelligence is not only analytics and already offers Follow Ups, Team Tasks, engagement, scheduling, and broader practice performance functions.

The category distinction is:
broad all-in-one practice performance platform vs narrow follow-up recovery layer.

## Claim Audit

- No universal superiority claims.
- No fake pricing comparison.
- No fake integrations.
- No fake customer case studies.
- No fake recovered revenue.
- No unsupported HIPAA claims.
- No competitor review quote is copied into public content.
- Review pain patterns are transformed into neutral evaluation questions.
- Simulated workflow data is labeled as demo data.

## Next SEO Work

Do not immediately create 20 more competitor pages.

After deployment:

1. Let Search Console discover the pages.
2. Monitor query impressions for 2–4 weeks.
3. Continue community and review mining.
4. Only add competitor pages when one of these is true:
   - real query impressions appear,
   - repeated community complaints reveal a stable pain pattern,
   - a competitor repeatedly appears in buyer conversations,
   - a partner or pilot explicitly compares that product.

Likely later candidates:

- NexHealth vs Follow-Up Recovery
- Adit vs Follow-Up Recovery
- Patient Prism vs Follow-Up Recovery
- Dentra alternatives — only after Praxora has stronger pilot proof
