The same test on every page

Whichever guide you open, the operating questions stay the same:

  1. Is the patient state still true, or did they already schedule, decline, or ask to wait?
  2. Why did follow-up stop?
  3. What is the next action, and what date is on it?
  4. Who owns that action?
  5. What outcome was recorded, separately from “we sent a message”?

Praxora is built for that recovery loop, with human review. It is not intake software, a virtual waiting room, or a post-visit survey. Frequently asked questions

What should a dental follow-up software alternative be compared on?

Compare the workflow, not the logo count. Ask whether each open case has a current state, a reason follow-up stopped, a next action, one owner, human review when a person is needed, and a recorded outcome.

Which alternatives does this page actually link?

Published guides cover MaxAssist, RecallMax, Weave, Dental Intelligence, AI dental receptionists, missed-call text-back, dental recall software, and automated follow-up versus a call list. Solutionreach, Lighthouse 360, and RevenueWell are not published here yet.

Can we compare tools before we integrate Dentrix or Open Dental?

Yes. Use aggregate counts in the revenue leak calculator. No patient-level data and no practice-management connection are required to estimate which leak to review first.
