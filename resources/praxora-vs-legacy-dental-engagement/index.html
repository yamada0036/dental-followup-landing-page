Praxora vs. Legacy Dental Patient Engagement Platforms

Your practice may already send reminders and see unscheduled patients. The question is what happens when the front desk has no time to work every open case. Compare the handoff from a signal to an owner, next action and recorded outcome.

Short answer: MaxAssist, RevenueWell, Lighthouse 360 and Solutionreach offer different mixes of reminders, recall, patient messaging, reporting and follow-up tools. They are not interchangeable, and several do more than send reminder texts. Praxora's proposed recovery layer focuses on how a practice reviews an unresolved case, assigns the next action and records what actually happened. This page is an evaluation guide, not a claim that the proposed Praxora workflow is already live or that another vendor lacks a particular feature.

Where the named platforms fit

  • MaxAssist: offers unscheduled treatment, recall and cancellation recovery features. Evaluate its prioritization, staff workflow and call coaching in the bundle you would buy.
  • RevenueWell: includes treatment plan follow-up campaigns and patient lists with call or text actions. Ask what a nonreply triggers and who owns it.
  • Lighthouse 360: emphasizes automated communication, reminders and recall. Test what staff see after a patient does not respond.
  • Solutionreach: offers automated recall, appointment reminders and two-way texting. Test how your office assigns unresolved conversations and measures booked visits.

Using one of these products does not mean you must replace it. The operational gap may sit between a message, a list and the next staffed action. For a broader set of vendor-specific guides, see the alternatives hub.

A five-minute test for your current setup

  1. Choose one patient who has unscheduled treatment and did not reply to a reminder. Is the current status accurate?
  2. Can the coordinator see the last contact, reason for delay and patient preference in one place?
  3. Who owns the next action, and when is it due?
  4. If the patient replies with a cost or clinical question, who reviews it before another message goes out?
  5. Can you distinguish “text sent” from “appointment booked” and “care completed”?

If the answer depends on someone remembering to call the list between check-ins, document that handoff first. A second campaign does not by itself finish the conversation.

Start with a working batch

Export a short, current list from Open Dental or Dentrix, verify each case and assign one owner. Use the five staff-reviewed text examples only where messaging is appropriate. Estimate exposure with the calculator, which needs aggregate inputs only.
