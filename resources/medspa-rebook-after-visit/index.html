Rebook After a Medspa Visit

The visit already happened. Someone recommended a return window, and the next appointment is not on the book. That is due rebook. It is not an unused package, and it is not a lapsed membership, unless a person checks and finds that those are also true.

Free Follow-Up Leak ReviewAll Medspa Recovery Guides

Checkout is where this leak either closes or starts. If the guest leaves without a next time, the operating system still has the completed visit. Zenoti or Boulevard can store that visit. Neither fact assigns an owner. This page is the queue for return windows that are already documented and still unbooked. The rest of the cluster is medspa follow-up recovery.

In a dental practice the matching list is recall: the patient was seen, the next visit was expected, and the list grew without an outcome. See recall recovery. Do not copy clinical recall intervals onto spa services. Use only the window your own provider or service plan already wrote down.

Find through recover

  1. Find. Completed visits with a documented return window and no future appointment. If your system cannot show the window, the row is not due yet. It is missing a note.
  2. Classify. Guest declined to book at checkout, guest asked for a later date, staff never asked, or unknown.
  3. Prioritize. Windows that are already here or arrive within 14 days, then older misses with no touch. Do not call a guest whose window is still months away just to fill a slow day.
  4. Human review. Confirm the visit happened, the window is real, and a future visit was not booked under another service name.
  5. Follow up. One owner, at checkout if they are still there, or one callback if they are not. Offer times. Record a decline.
  6. Recover. Rebooked, declined, callback on a date, unreachable, or not a candidate. A checkout text with no reply is still open.

Who is actually due

Checkout versus the callback

At checkout, if a window was stated: "The note says the next visit is around [window]. I have [time] and [time]. If you would rather wait, tell me the week to call you and I will put my name on it."

If they already left: "This is [Name] at [Spa]. After your visit, the next time was not booked. The note we have says around [window]. I can offer [time] or [time]. If that window is wrong, say so and I will correct the record instead of guessing."

Do not add treatment detail, before-and-after claims, or a price you have not checked. If they decline, record declined. A decline is a finished outcome. Chasing it with a new offer the same day is a second campaign, and it needs its own owner if you run one.

Count due rebooks, in aggregate, in the overdue-recall fields of the dental revenue leak calculator if you want a sketch beside missed calls and unused-package value. Keep lapsed memberships as a separate count. The labels on the calculator stay dental. That is intentional. Medspa teams are welcome on the free follow-up leak review: describe this list in the recall box, say you are a medspa, and do not include guest names.

Frequently asked questions

When is a medspa guest due to rebook?

When a provider or the service plan already named a return window and no future visit is on the book. A guest who was not given a return window is not automatically due. Do not invent the interval to fill the schedule.

Should checkout book the next visit every time?

Checkout is the best moment when the guest is standing there and a return window was already stated. If they leave without booking, the row still needs an owner. A reminder campaign does not replace that owner.

How should a medspa count due rebooks in the Praxora calculator?

As an aggregate count in the overdue-recall fields. Keep lapsed memberships as their own count if you enter both, and do not add the two into one recovered-revenue claim. The calculator remains a dental tool used as a sketch.
