Unused packages

Medspa Unused Package Follow-Up

An unused package or an open consult is the medspa version of unscheduled treatment. The guest already committed, or already came in to talk, and the next visit was never booked. The operating system can show the balance. Someone still has to own the next action.

Prepaid value with no appointment is a leak, not a loyalty metric. Zenoti, Boulevard, or your current system of record is where the package lives. This page does not replace that system and does not claim an integration. It is the working batch: which rows are still open, who calls, and what outcome gets written down. The map of the other leaks is medspa follow-up recovery.

Dental teams do this from a treatment report. The sort they use is value, expiry, insurance remaining, and touch history, after they build a working batch. A medspa uses the same shape with different fields. There is no insurance-remaining column unless you actually have one.

Find through recover

  1. Find. Pull packages with remaining visits or remaining prepaid value and no future appointment. Pull open consults that never booked as a separate count.
  2. Classify. Waiting on a date the guest already gave, could not find a time, asked a question nobody answered, or unknown.
  3. Prioritize. Documented expiration first, then remaining value your books already show, then longest silence. Details are below.
  4. Human review. Confirm the balance, that no visit is already booked, and that the guest did not ask you to wait. One guest with three packages becomes one conversation, not three calls.
  5. Follow up. One owner offers times or sets a callback. Do not quote a balance you have not checked today. Do not describe a clinical result.
  6. Recover. Booked, declined, waiting until a date, unreachable, expired under your written terms, or not a candidate. A reminder blast is not an outcome.

How to sort the first batch

Use dates and dollars the system already stores. Do not invent an expiration to make a row feel urgent.
FactorThis week's batchLeave for later
ExpirationA date already written in the package terms, inside 30 days.No expiration on file. Do not create one for the sake of the list.
Remaining valueAmong in-window rows, higher remaining prepaid value first. Use the ledger, not a guessed retail price.A high balance that failed the other checks, including a guest who asked to wait.
Touch historyNo completed touch in 14 or more days, and no future callback.Contacted in the last 7 days, or a callback date that has not arrived.
Future visitNone on the book for this package.Already booked. Remove the row.

Cap the batch at about 20 rows, or fewer if fewer qualify. Twenty is capacity for one owner. It is not a quota. Dental offices use the same cap on the treatment priority page.

If they answer: "This is [Name] at [Spa]. You still have visits left on your package and I do not see a next time on the book. I can offer [time] or [time]. If you want to wait, tell me the date I should call."

Do not name a clinical finding. Do not say the unused balance will "expire" unless that date is in the terms a person just confirmed.

Open consults

An open consult is the same leak with a smaller dollar field: the guest came, a next visit was discussed, and nothing was booked. Keep consults in this workflow. Do not bury them in a marketing list. If the consult recommended a series that was never purchased, the outcome might be "declined" or "callback on a date," not "unused package."

A guest who already had the visit and was told when to return, with no package balance involved, belongs on rebook after a visit. A no-show against a package visit belongs on no-show recovery until a person sees they are the same appointment.

For an aggregate sketch only, put unused-package or open-consult value in the unscheduled-treatment fields of the dental revenue leak calculator. Say what you mapped. The calculator will not relabel itself for a medspa, and the output is not a forecast. Frequently asked questions

Is an unused package the same problem as unscheduled dental treatment?

It is the same operating problem: intent already exists, the next visit is not booked, and nobody owns the follow-up. It is not the same clinical workflow, and it does not make Praxora a medspa product. The dental workflow is unscheduled treatment follow-up.

How should a medspa order the first batch of unused packages?

Confirm the package is still open and has no future visit. Prefer rows with a documented expiration already in your terms, then remaining prepaid value the books already show, then the longest gap since the last touch. Cap the batch at what one owner can finish. Do not invent an expiration date.

Can unused-package value go into the Praxora calculator?

Only as an aggregate stand-in for the unscheduled-treatment input. The calculator is built for dental practices. The number is a sketch of which pile is large, not a medspa forecast. Do not upload guest names or package ledgers.
