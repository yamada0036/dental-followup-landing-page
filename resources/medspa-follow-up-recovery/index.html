Medspa Follow-Up Recovery for Missed Calls, No-Shows, and Unused Packages

Zenoti and Boulevard can run the spa. They do not, by themselves, give every open leak an owner and an outcome. This cluster is that recovery layer: missed calls, no-shows, unused packages, lapsed memberships, and visits that were never rebooked.

This is not a second product and it is not medspa software. Praxora's published service is dental follow-up recovery for missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall. A medspa has the same operating failure in different lists. A guest called and nobody owns the callback. A package still has visits and no next appointment. A membership lapsed, or a provider said "come back in six weeks," and the row never moved.

The dental version of that problem is follow-up leakage. The steps do not change: find, classify, prioritize, human review, follow up, recover.

Not an operating system

Zenoti and Boulevard are full operating systems: booking, point of sale, packages, memberships, and the client record. If one of them is already the system of record, keep it. These pages do not replace it, do not price it, and do not claim a Praxora integration with it.

The gap they leave is the row that is visible and still unresolved. A report of unused packages is not a recovery workflow until each row has a current state, one owner, a next action with a date, and an outcome that is more specific than "we sent a text."

Unused packages and open consults are the unscheduled-treatment analogue: the intent already exists, and nobody booked the next visit. Lapsed memberships and due rebooks are the recall analogue: the relationship already exists, and the next visit has no owner. Do not merge those two recall-shaped lists. A frozen membership the guest asked for is not the same as a guest who finished a visit and never rebooked.

The same six steps

  1. Find. Start from the list the operating system already has. Do not build a second database of guest names for this exercise.
  2. Classify. Why did it stop? No answer, asked to wait, package expired under your own terms, membership frozen on request, no-show, or unknown.
  3. Prioritize. A short batch the coordinator can finish. Expiry of a package or membership, remaining prepaid value, and days since last touch are operational sorts. They are not a clinical or aesthetic urgency score.
  4. Human review. A person confirms the row is still open before anyone calls. Reports go stale.
  5. Follow up. One owner, one next action, one date. No procedure details in a voicemail.
  6. Recover. Record booked, declined, waiting, unreachable, or closed. A sent message is not an outcome.

Dental offices use the same sequence on unscheduled treatment, missed calls, and recall. The medspa pages stay on the medspa lists.

How a medspa can start

Medspa teams are welcome on the free follow-up leak review. The form uses dental labels. In the unscheduled-treatment box, describe unused packages or open consults. In the recall box, describe lapsed memberships or guests who are due to rebook. In the PMS box, name Zenoti, Boulevard, or whatever you already use. Do not include guest names, phone numbers, or treatment details.

The revenue leak calculator is a dental tool with three inputs: missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall. A medspa can use it only as an aggregate sketch. Put missed-call counts in the missed-call fields, unused-package or open-consult value in the unscheduled-treatment fields, and lapsed memberships or due rebooks in the recall fields. The result is not a medspa forecast. It does not create a second product. It shows which of those three piles is large enough to review first. Leave guest-level data out.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a Zenoti or Boulevard alternative?

No. Zenoti and Boulevard are full medspa operating systems. These pages are a recovery workflow for rows that stay open inside the system you already use: an owner, a next action, and a recorded outcome. They do not replace booking, point of sale, memberships, or charts, and they do not claim an integration.

Does Praxora have a separate medspa product?

No. Praxora's published service is dental follow-up recovery. The same operating questions apply to a medspa. The free workflow review welcomes medspa teams. The calculator keeps its dental labels and can only be used as an aggregate sketch.

Which leak should a medspa work first?

Pick one list the team can finish: missed calls, no-shows, unused packages, lapsed memberships, or guests who were due to rebook and did not. Give that list an owner and an outcome before you open a second list.
