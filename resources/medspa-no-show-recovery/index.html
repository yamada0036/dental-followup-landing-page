Medspa No-Show Recovery

A no-show is a visit that was booked and did not happen. Recovery is the next owned step: confirm it was a real miss, decide whether to rebook, and record the outcome. A cancellation fee, if you charge one, is your policy. It is not this workflow.

The book already knew they were coming. Zenoti, Boulevard, or whichever system holds the appointment can mark the no-show. The leak is the empty next action. Nobody rebooked, nobody closed it, and the same guest may also be sitting on an unused package. Those are related rows. They are not the same row until a person checks. Find through recover

  1. Find. Use today's no-show or cancellation list from the system you already run. Same-day rows first.
  2. Classify. Forgot, said they rescheduled, provider asked them not to come, payment failed, or unknown. Do not invent a reason to make the list look tidy.
  3. Prioritize. Open same-day misses the front desk can still rebook, then recent misses with no next appointment. A guest who already rebooked leaves the list.
  4. Human review. Confirm they did not arrive, did not move the visit, and did not ask not to be contacted. Check the package or membership only to avoid a second conflicting call.
  5. Follow up. One owner offers a new time or sets a callback. Do not lead with a fee unless that person is authorized under a policy you already published.
  6. Recover. Rebooked, declined, fee applied under your policy, unreachable, or closed. "Marked no-show" is the start, not the outcome.

Classify the miss

A short rebook line, if they answer: "This is [Name] at [Spa]. We were expecting you for your visit today and I want to get you back on the book. I have [time] and [time]. If neither works, tell me a window and I will own the follow-up."

Stop if they raise a clinical question or a complaint. Name the person who should take it and change the owner.

Keep it separate from other leaks

A no-show guest with visits left on a package still needs the package checked, but the package follow-up is unused package follow-up. A guest who came and did not book the next visit is rebook after a visit, not a no-show. A membership that lapsed is lapsed membership recovery.

There is no no-show field on the dental calculator. Do not stuff no-show counts into unscheduled treatment or recall and then read the result as a spa forecast. If you want a workflow review, medspa teams are welcome on the free follow-up leak review. Put the no-show path in the notes about which workflow is most frustrating. Do not include guest names.

Frequently asked questions

Is a no-show the same as an unused package?

No. A no-show is a visit that was on the book and did not happen. An unused package is prepaid value with no future visit. The same guest can be on both lists. Work them as two rows unless a person confirms they are the same appointment.

Should recovery start with a cancellation fee?

Only if the spa already has a fee policy and a person is supposed to apply it. This guide does not set a fee. The recovery question is whether the visit was rebooked, declined, or closed, and who owns that step.

How fast should a medspa call a no-show?

The same day when the schedule still has a hole and the guest might rebook into it. If same-day contact does not happen, the row still needs an owner and a next date. A late call is a process failure only if nobody owns it.
