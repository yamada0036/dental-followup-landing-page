Lighthouse 360 and follow-up recovery are not the same category

Henry Schein One describes Lighthouse 360 as patient communication that automates reminders and confirmations, asks for reviews, sends recall reminders, and supports scheduling and pre-visit forms. That is a communication and patient-experience product. A practice shopping for it is often trying to cut no-shows and stop building every reminder by hand.

Follow-up recovery assumes the opportunity is already known. A call was missed. Treatment was discussed and not booked. A recall patient is overdue after the usual reminders. The next question is operational: why did it stop, who owns it, and what outcome was recorded? That problem is follow-up leakage. The same category line, for a different communication platform, is Weave vs follow-up recovery.

What Lighthouse 360 is designed to help with

Public pages, reviewed on 1 November 2026, describe a dental communication product. Henry Schein One’s product page emphasizes automated reminders and confirmations by text, email, and phone, review and feedback requests, recall reminders, and communication that stays tied to the schedule. Lighthouse’s own features pages add continuing-care reminders when a patient is due for a cleaning, reminders for dormant patients, procedure-based messages, and a cancellation fill-in text. Two-way texting and front-desk task lists are described on Lighthouse 360+. A separate public page describes treatment-plan reminders that can stop when treatment is scheduled.

Those are the vendor’s descriptions, not a measured result for any practice. This page does not repeat percentage claims from marketing articles. What a follow-up recovery layer is designed to help with

Praxora is built to turn known leaks into one daily queue for the front desk:

  • A missed call that still has no owner after the first text or voicemail.
  • Unscheduled treatment, including a chairside reason such as price, a spouse, or next month.
  • Overdue recall that remains open after the standard reminders.

Staff approve messages. The workflow is meant to support the team, not replace the front desk or the treatment coordinator. Praxora is early. It does not publish customer stories or a subscription price. It does not replace reminders, forms, or review software, and it does not diagnose.

The recall half of that list is recall leakage. The treatment half is unscheduled treatment follow-up. The call half is missed call recovery.

Reminders are one boundary

Lighthouse 360’s recall and confirmation tools are designed to contact patients who are due, overdue, or already on the book. A confirmation that lands in the practice-management system saves a manual update. That is useful, and it is not the same as a recovery case.

The recovery question begins when the patient does not book or does not confirm:

  • Is the current recall status still true?
  • Did someone already call?
  • Is there a next date, or only another reminder?
  • Who owns the row this week?

See dental recall software vs recall recovery for that distinction without a vendor name attached.

Treatment-plan reminders are another boundary

Lighthouse publicly describes treatment-plan reminders aimed at patients with unscheduled treatment, including messages that can stop once treatment is scheduled. That is a communication rule. It does not, by itself, record why the patient hesitated.

A patient who said the fee was the problem should not automatically get the same next step as a patient who asked to decide next month or to talk with a spouse. Praxora is designed to keep that chairside intent on the case the front desk works. How those reasons change the action is on treatment follow-up by patient intent. Message wording the team can edit is on unscheduled treatment text samples.

Compare the system of record for the next action

Front-desk task lists inside a communication product can show missing forms, birthdays, or other prep. A recovery queue should show a different set of fields: current state, why follow-up stopped, last relevant touch, next action, owner, and outcome. Ask which screen the team opens at the start of the day when the work is “patients who did not book.”

If that answer is already clear inside Lighthouse 360, you may not need another layer. If the answer is a spreadsheet next to the reminder history, the layer you are missing is ownership, not another broadcast. Activity and outcome are different counts. Praxora’s working stages are found, prioritized, approved, contacted, replied, booked, and recovered. “Approved” is there because a person reviews the next message.

When Lighthouse 360 may be the better fit

Lighthouse 360 may be the better fit when the practice wants Henry Schein One’s communication tools: reminders, confirmations, recall messages, review requests, forms, and the related scheduling outreach. A focused recovery product should not pretend to cover that list.

It may also be the better fit when the practice is already standardizing on that vendor’s other systems and wants patient messaging in the same family. When a recovery layer may be worth evaluating

Evaluate a recovery layer when reminders are already running and the unresolved work is still informal. The front desk cannot see, in one place, which missed call, which unscheduled plan, and which overdue recall patient needs a person today. Priority is a guess. Outcomes are “we sent something.”

Can they work together?

Conceptually, yes. Lighthouse 360 can keep sending reminders, confirmations, and recall messages. A recovery workflow can hold the cases that remain open and record what a person did next. This page does not claim that integration exists.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lighthouse 360 designed to do?

Henry Schein One describes Lighthouse 360 as dental patient communication: automated reminders and confirmations, recall reminders, review requests, and pre-visit forms. Its own features pages also describe continuing-care outreach, dormant-patient reminders, treatment-plan reminders, and two-way texting on Lighthouse 360+.

Does follow-up recovery replace Lighthouse 360?

No. A recovery workflow does not replace reminders, confirmations, forms, or review requests. Praxora is an early product built for unresolved missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall, with staff approving messages.

Can Lighthouse 360 and a recovery queue coexist?

Conceptually, yes. Communication software can send the reminders while a recovery queue tracks which opportunities are still open. This page does not claim a live Praxora integration with Lighthouse 360.
