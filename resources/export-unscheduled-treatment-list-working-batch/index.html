Export Your Unscheduled List and Build a Working Batch (Dentrix / Open Dental)

Export the list, de-dupe it, separate S and U from treatment that is still open, then give every remaining row one owner and an outcome column. The export is a snapshot. The working batch is the thing the team can finish.

Four moves, in this order: export, de-dupe S and U, name an owner, record an outcome. Offices stall between the first and the second. The file looks finished because it came out of Dentrix or Open Dental. It is not finished until each row has a person and a place to write what happened.

Click-paths live on the source pages: how to pull the Dentrix unscheduled treatment report and how to pull the Open Dental unscheduled treatment report. This page is what you do with the output. When the rows are clean, sort them with value, expiry, insurance remaining, and touch history.

Export

Use the report your version actually produces. Do not wait on a CSV button that your build may not have.

  • Dentrix desktop. Office Manager, Reports, Lists, Unscheduled Treatment Plans sends the list to the Batch Processor. Treatment Manager is the filterable view from the Appointment Book. Confirm the menu in your version. Dentrix Ascend does not use that Office Manager path. The full notes are on the Dentrix report page.
  • Open Dental. Standard Reports, Lists, Treatment Finder. Leave "Include patients with upcoming appointments" unchecked when you want an unscheduled list. Refresh, review, then Export to File. The manual link and the filter notes are on the Treatment Finder page.

Write the source and the export date on the working file before you edit anything. A list from last month is not a list from this morning. Patients schedule, complete, and decline treatment after the Batch Processor finishes.

If you are not ready to pull either report, count the open cases and use aggregate numbers only. The free follow-up leak review asks how the list is worked. It does not ask for the file.

De-dupe, including S and U

Clean the export before you assign owners. Otherwise two people call the same patient, or someone calls a patient whose visit is already booked.

S and U are Dentrix list-report marks, from Dentrix Help. Open Dental does not use those letters. Confirm appointment status in the chart either way.

Where the file lives

The working batch contains patient references. Leave it in the system your practice already approves for that information. Do not email the export to a personal inbox, and do not upload it to the public calculator.

The dental revenue leak calculator asks how many presented plans are still unscheduled and, if you know it, a rough aggregate value. That is enough to see whether this leak is the one to review first. A later Dentrix or Open Dental connection is useful when stale appointment status becomes the main mistake. Frequently asked questions

Does an S or a U on the Dentrix report mean the treatment is scheduled?

No. Dentrix Help uses S for an appointment for other treatment-planned work, and U for an appointment on the Unscheduled List. A blank means no scheduled appointment and no Unscheduled List appointment. Confirm the chart and the Appointment Book before anyone calls.

Should every procedure line become its own call?

No. Collapse a patient to one working row for the plan the team will discuss together. Calling once per exported procedure line is how the same person hears from the office three times in a day.

Do we have to upload the export to start?

No. Keep the working file inside the practice's approved systems. The calculator and the free workflow review use aggregate counts only. They do not ask for names, chart numbers, or the export file.
