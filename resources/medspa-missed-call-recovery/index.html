Medspa Missed-Call Recovery

A missed call at a medspa is open until someone owns the callback and writes down what happened. The phone system, the text-back, or the operating system can show that the call existed. Recovery starts after that.

Catching the ring and recovering the guest are different jobs. Zenoti, Boulevard, a VoIP system, or an answering agent may log the miss or send a text. This page does not replace those tools and does not claim an integration. It covers the row that is still unanswered: who calls back, what they may say, and which outcome closes it. Find through recover

  1. Find. Start from the call log you already have. Drop spam, duplicates, and calls that were already returned.
  2. Classify. New guest, existing guest wanting a time, question about a package or membership, or unknown. Unknown stays unknown. Do not infer a service from a missed call.
  3. Prioritize. Same-day misses with no callback first, then older unresolved misses. A guest who already booked does not stay in the queue.
  4. Human review. Confirm the number, any do-not-call note, and that nobody else on the team is already calling.
  5. Follow up. One owner. One attempt path: no answer, voicemail, or a live callback.
  6. Recover. Booked, declined, already handled, needs a coordinator, or unable to reach. "Text sent" is not on that list.

Dental practices use the same split between an answering layer and a recovery workflow. The comparisons are AI receptionist vs follow-up recovery and text-back vs recovery. Spoken lines written for a dental front desk are on dental missed-call follow-up scripts. Use the shape. Do not copy dental treatment language onto a medspa call.

What the callback may say

Voicemail: "Hello, this is [Name] at [Spa]. You called us and we missed you. You can reach the front desk at [number] during [hours]. Please ask for [Name]. I will not leave service or pricing details on this voicemail."

Live answer: "Hello, this is [Name] at [Spa]. Am I speaking with the person who called? We missed you. I can help with a time, or I can have the right person call you. What were you calling about?"

  • If they want a time, offer two openings you can see, or take a window and call back. Do not invent a slot.
  • If they ask about a price, a package balance, or a clinical question, name the person who owns that answer and set a time. Do not guess.
  • If there is no answer, log the attempt. One voicemail per callback cycle unless your own policy says otherwise. Set the next attempt or close it as unable to reach after the limit you wrote down.

Frequently asked questions

Does an AI receptionist or a text-back close a medspa missed call?

It can answer or acknowledge the first miss. The call is still open until someone owns the next step and records booked, declined, already handled, or unable to reach.

What belongs in a medspa callback voicemail?

Your name, the spa name, that you missed their call, and how to reach the front desk. Do not leave service names, prices, or medical detail on voicemail.

Where should a medspa put missed-call counts if it uses the Praxora calculator?

In the missed-call fields. The calculator is labeled for dental practices. Using it this way is an aggregate sketch, not a medspa forecast. Do not enter guest names.
