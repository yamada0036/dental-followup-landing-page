Medspa Lapsed Membership Recovery

A lapsed membership is the medspa version of an overdue recall list. The relationship already exists. The status changed, and nobody owns the next step. A freeze the guest asked for is not a lapse to win back.

Free Follow-Up Leak ReviewAll Medspa Recovery Guides

Membership status is not a recovery workflow. Zenoti, Boulevard, and similar systems store whether a membership is active, past due, frozen, or cancelled. Keep that as the source. This page is the queue for rows that are open and unowned. It is not a second membership product, and Praxora is not launching one. The cluster home is medspa follow-up recovery.

Dental recall has the same shape: a list of people who were expected back, without an owner or an outcome. That comparison is recall lists vs recall recovery and recall software vs recovery.

Find through recover

  1. Find. Export or filter memberships that are past due, cancelled, or frozen past the date the guest agreed. Leave active members who simply have not visited this week off this list. They may belong on rebook after a visit if a return date was missed.
  2. Classify. Past due with no contact, cancelled and unconfirmed, frozen on request, frozen past the resume date, or unknown.
  3. Prioritize. Past-due rows with a documented bill date in the next 30 days, then cancellations nobody has confirmed, then freezes that already passed their resume date. Honor a future resume date.
  4. Human review. Read the note. A guest who cancelled in person yesterday should not get a win-back call today. A failed card is a billing owner, not a generic recall script.
  5. Follow up. One owner. The ask is a status, not a lecture: update the card, confirm the cancel, or set the resume visit.
  6. Recover. Reactivated, cancel confirmed, frozen until a date, unreachable, or closed. "Email sent" does not reactivate a membership.

Read the status before you call

Call only the statuses a person has checked. Do not treat every non-visit as a lapse.

If they answer a past-due row: "This is [Name] at [Spa]. Your membership shows past due and I want to make sure the next step is clear. I can take an updated card, or I can confirm that you want it cancelled. Which should I record?"

Do not invent a save offer. If the spa has a written save policy, only the person authorized to use it should say it, and the outcome still has to be recorded.

A batch, not the whole roster

Work a batch one owner can finish, on the order above. A full cancelled-member export going back years is a archive, not a queue. Recent status changes with no touch come first.

Unused prepaid visits that are not a membership status problem stay on unused package follow-up. Missed calls about a membership charge start on missed-call recovery and move here only when the owner becomes the person who can change the membership.

On the dental calculator, a count of lapsed memberships can sit in the overdue-recall fields as an aggregate sketch. Do not add it to unused-package value and call the sum recovered revenue. The form will still say recall. That is a label, not a new product. For a person to review the process, medspa teams are welcome on the free follow-up leak review. Use the recall box. Frequently asked questions

Is a frozen medspa membership a lapsed membership?

Not if the guest asked for the freeze and the resume date is still in the future. Park the row until that date. A past-due or cancelled membership with no owner is the lapse to review.

How is lapsed membership recovery different from rebooking after a visit?

Lapsed membership recovery starts from membership status: past due, cancelled, or frozen past the agreed date. Rebook-after-visit starts from a completed visit whose next appointment was never booked. A guest can be on one list without being on the other. The rebook guide is rebook after a visit.

Where do lapsed memberships go in the dental calculator?

As an aggregate count in the overdue-recall fields, and only as a sketch. The calculator is a dental tool. It does not forecast membership revenue. Do not enter member names.
