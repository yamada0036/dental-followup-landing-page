What Demandforce publicly describes

Demandforce’s own pages describe a patient engagement platform. The public feature set includes appointment reminders by email, text, and voice, two-way texting, automated recall aimed at inactive or lapsed patients, review tools, and email campaigns such as newsletters and “we miss you” reactivation. Reminders are described across the visit: before the appointment and after it, to encourage the next booking.

That is a marketing and communication job. It can sit on top of the practice-management schedule and send the messages a team would otherwise dial by hand. This page does not treat older Demandforce survey statistics as current performance, and it does not invent a price. The descriptions were checked against public pages on 1 November 2026. Ask for the dental package and the first-year total in writing.

Demandforce is not Lighthouse 360

Both names show up in dental communication searches, and both have been discussed in connection with larger dental-software groups. They are still separate products with separate sites. A feature on one page is not a feature on the other. If the product you have is Lighthouse 360, use Lighthouse 360 vs follow-up recovery instead of this page.

The same care applies to other engagement names. RevenueWell and Solutionreach also send recall and reminders. Weave adds a phone platform. None of those pages is a Demandforce clone.

Reactivation campaigns versus an owned case

An automated recall or reactivation campaign is aimed at a segment: patients with no recent visit, or patients due for a checkup. The campaign succeeds when some of them book. It does not, by itself, tell the front desk which remaining patients are worth a personal call this morning, or why the last attempt stopped.

A follow-up recovery case is one row:

  • Current state. Still overdue, already scheduled, or asked to wait.
  • Why follow-up stopped, if the practice knows.
  • The last relevant touch, so the team does not repeat it blindly.
  • The next action and the date.
  • One owner.
  • An outcome other than “email sent.”

Praxora is built for that row across missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall. Staff approve the message. The queue is designed to make the existing team faster, not to remove the front desk. Praxora is early. There are no customer counts on this page. The general form of the recall question, without a vendor, is recall software vs recall recovery.

Known leaks and chairside intent

Demandforce-style reactivation is a strong fit for patients who have gone quiet. It is a weaker fit, unless you confirm otherwise in a demo, for a patient who was in the chair last week and stated a reason. Price was the concern. They wanted to talk with a spouse. They asked for next month.

Those three should not share one reactivation email. Praxora is designed to put that chairside intent on the daily queue, next to the other known leaks: a missed call and an overdue recall patient. The intent guide is treatment follow-up by patient intent. Pulling the unscheduled list from the system you already run is Dentrix or Open Dental, then a working batch.

Missed calls are the third leak. A reminder platform may never see them. The callback, after the miss is already a fact, is dental missed call recovery.

When Demandforce may be the better fit

Choose a patient-engagement platform, Demandforce or another, when the practice wants reminders, recall campaigns, review requests, and email marketing from one vendor. Praxora does not offer that suite. Switching to a recovery workflow will not publish your newsletter or answer review alerts.

Also stay when the campaigns are fine and the gap is that no one works the non-responders. An alternative cannot invent a named owner. If you are comparing MaxAssist and Dental Intelligence specifically for appointment recall, that head-to-head is MaxAssist vs Dental Intelligence for appointment recall. When a recovery layer may be worth evaluating

A recovery layer is the better first conversation when engagement mail is already going out and three lists are still informal: missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall. The team can send a message. It cannot say, for a single patient, what is true now and what happens next.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of product is Demandforce?

Demandforce publicly describes a patient engagement platform with appointment reminders, automated recall for inactive patients, two-way texting, review tools, and email campaigns such as reactivation. Confirm the current dental package before you treat that list as your subscription.

Is Demandforce the same product as Lighthouse 360?

No. They are separate products with their own sites. Lighthouse 360 is compared on its own page. Do not assume a feature on one is a feature on the other.

Is Praxora a Demandforce alternative for every job?

No. Praxora does not replace reminders, review marketing, or newsletters. It is an early follow-up recovery workflow for missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall. Staff approve messages. It is designed to support the front desk, not replace it.
