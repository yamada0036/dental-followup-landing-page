A Missed Implant Consultation Is One Example of Follow-Up Leakage

This page uses an example scenario to show how patient intent can fall out of a normal follow-up process.

See Where Your Follow-Up Process May Be LeakingRead the pillar guide
Example Scenario. This is not a customer case study, not a claim of actual recovered revenue, and not based on real patient data.

Where the example leaks

A potential implant patient calls during a busy front desk window. The call is missed. A callback happens later, but the patient does not answer. No second attempt is owned, no context is classified, and no outcome is tracked.

How to reframe the workflow

  • Find the missed call
  • Classify likely context
  • Prioritize the opportunity
  • Human review
  • Follow up
  • Recover or close the outcome

Why this matters

The missed call is only the first event. The larger leak appears when the opportunity has no owner, no next action, and no tracked result. For the broader operational definition, learn more about follow-up leakage.
