What is Praxora?

Praxora is dental follow-up software, with human review. Praxora operates it at www.getpraxora.com.

What Praxora is

Praxora is software for dental practices to recover missed calls and follow up with patients. The workflows published on this site are missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall. Each open case is meant to have an owner, a next action, and a recorded outcome.

What the software does

The product is AI-assisted. This site describes it as a human-reviewed AI assist for routing and approved outreach. The published steps are find, classify, prioritize, human review, follow up, and recover.

In practice, that means an open missed call, an unscheduled treatment case, or an overdue recall patient is worked as a case: someone at the practice owns the next action, and the outcome is recorded. Practice staff review the next step before outreach.

Software, with human review

Praxora is software with human review before a message is sent. It is not an agency, a managed service, or a done-for-you follow-up operation. A person at the practice reviews the next step. The free workflow review on this site is a separate conversation about the practice’s process, using aggregate numbers. It does not contact patients.

Praxora does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinical judgment. It does not replace patient intake, a virtual waiting room, or post-visit surveys. The free calculator and the free workflow review do not require patient-level data or a practice-management connection. Live patient outreach and PMS synchronization depend on a separately agreed setup.

Who operates it

Praxora, the company behind this site, operates the software. The site is https://www.getpraxora.com.

The same summary is on the homepage. Privacy questions go to the address on the privacy page.
