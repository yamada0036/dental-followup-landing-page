Privacy

This page explains how Praxora handles practice-level information submitted through the website.

Information You Provide

You may provide first name, email, practice name, practice-level workflow estimates, and follow-up workflow descriptions.

Do not submit patient names, clinical records, or other patient-level health information through these forms.

How Information Is Used

Praxora uses the information you provide to deliver calculator recovery breakdowns, provide requested recovery resources, respond to Follow-Up Leak Review requests, understand which workflows users are interested in, and improve the website and calculator.

Calculator Data

The Revenue Leak Calculator uses practice-level estimates such as call volume, follow-up process consistency, unscheduled treatment estimates, and overdue recall counts.

The calculator does not require patient-level information.

Follow-Up Leak Review Data

The Free Follow-Up Leak Review form asks about practice-level workflows for missed calls, unscheduled treatment, and overdue recall so Praxora can review where follow-up may be breaking.

Analytics

We use website analytics to understand how visitors use pages and features such as the Revenue Leak Calculator.

Email Communication

When you request a recovery breakdown or workflow review, we use the email address you provide to deliver the requested information and communicate about that request.

Data Retention

Praxora retains submitted practice-level information for as long as needed to respond to requests, understand website performance, and improve the product. You can ask for your information to be removed.
