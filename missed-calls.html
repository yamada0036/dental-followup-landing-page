How to Build a Missed Call Recovery Workflow for a Dental Practice

A missed call is not always the problem. Follow-up leakage starts when the missed call never enters a reliable recovery workflow.

Calculate Your Revenue LeakLearn about follow-up leakage
Core argument

The call log is not the workflow.

A missed call can be a new patient, a patient trying to reschedule, a treatment question, or an urgent concern. The leak begins when that signal is visible but not owned.

A reliable workflow should move the missed call through six operational steps:

  • Find
  • Classify
  • Prioritize
  • Assign or review
  • Follow up
  • Track booking outcomes

What to measure

  • How many calls are missed during business hours.
  • How quickly missed calls are identified.
  • Whether each missed call has a next action and owner.
  • Which calls turn into replies, bookings, or closed outcomes.

Where Praxora fits

Praxora is designed to help dental teams find missed-call opportunities, classify likely context, prioritize follow-up, keep human review in the loop, and track whether the patient moved toward booking.
