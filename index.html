Common questions

Dental patient follow-up, explained.

What is dental patient follow-up?

Dental patient follow-up is the process of contacting patients after an inquiry or visit to agree on the next step. It can include returning missed calls, checking whether a patient wants to schedule treatment already discussed with the practice, and contacting patients overdue for recall. Each opportunity needs an owner, a next action and a recorded outcome.

How is follow-up recovery different from appointment reminders?

Appointment reminders concern visits that are already booked. Follow-up recovery addresses unresolved opportunities, such as an unanswered inquiry, treatment that has not been scheduled or an overdue patient who has not returned. See our dental recall and recovery comparison.

Where should a dental practice start?

Choose one workflow: missed calls, unscheduled treatment or overdue recall. Review how opportunities are identified, who owns the next action and how replies and bookings are recorded.
