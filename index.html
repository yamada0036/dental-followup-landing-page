The leak usually starts after intent appears.

A patient calls, leaves without scheduling, or appears on an overdue list. The risk is not only the event itself. It is the lack of a next action, owner, and tracked outcome.

SignalPatient intent appears

A call, treatment plan, or recall status indicates a patient may need follow-up.

GapOwnership gets unclear
