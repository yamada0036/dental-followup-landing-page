Dental Recall Lists vs Recall Recovery Workflows

A recall list tells you who is overdue. A recovery workflow tells you what happens next. When the next action is inconsistent, follow-up leakage begins.

Calculate Your Revenue LeakGet a Free Follow-Up Leak Review

Why lists are not enough

Most practices can produce an overdue recall list. The operational issue is deciding who to prioritize, what message to use, when to retry, who owns the attempt, and how the outcome is tracked.

Recall recovery workflow

  • Find overdue patients
  • Segment by recall status
  • Prioritize reactivation opportunities
  • Review outreach
  • Follow up
  • Track reactivated patients

Where to start

Start by measuring how many overdue patients have a current owner and next action. Then calculate your estimated follow-up leakage or read the follow-up leakage pillar guide.
