# SEO Sprint 3 Research Notes

Research date: 2026-07-07

These notes are for internal editorial review. The public pages are deliberately written as workflow evaluation guides rather than unsupported superiority claims.

## MaxAssist / RecallMax

Official positioning reviewed:

- MaxAssist identifies itself as formerly RecallMax.
- Current public feature set includes MaxAi / daily action plans, prioritized patients, unscheduled treatment, hygiene & recall, missed pre-appointed patients, cancellation recovery, scheduling, patient communication, analytics, and other practice productivity tools.
- The official unscheduled treatment page states that prioritization considers timing, past outreach, and task history and describes automatic removal of patients who are already scheduled.

Sources reviewed:

- https://maxassist.com/
- https://maxassist.com/features/
- https://maxassist.com/features/unscheduled-treatment/
- https://maxassist.com/compare-maxassist/

Review-pattern research:

G2 review summaries and individual review excerpts mention user-reported concerns around confusing follow-up notes, clarity of last-call / next-follow-up dates, messaging limitations, customization, and a Short Notice List state issue in at least one review.

The public Sprint 3 pages do not state that MaxAssist universally lacks these capabilities. Those pain patterns were converted into neutral evaluation questions such as current-state visibility, context reconstruction, next-action clarity, and outcome tracking.

Source reviewed:

- https://www.g2.com/products/maxassist/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons

## Weave

Official positioning reviewed:

- Weave is currently a broad communication platform.
- Dental pages include phones, two-way texting, missed-call texts, appointment reminders and confirmations, recall-related communication, payments, forms, analytics, and AI receptionist capabilities.
- Weave's current call-tracking content also discusses visibility into follow-up and outcomes.

Sources reviewed:

- https://www.getweave.com/
- https://www.getweave.com/industry/dentistry/
- https://www.getweave.com/ai-receptionist/
- https://www.getweave.com/dental-call-tracking/
- https://www.getweave.com/appointment-reminder-software/

Editorial implication:

The Sprint 3 comparison does not mischaracterize Weave as “just texting.” It frames the comparison as broad communication infrastructure vs a focused follow-up recovery layer.

## Dental Intelligence

Official positioning reviewed:

- Dental Intelligence is not only analytics.
- Current public pages include Follow Ups, Team Tasks, scorecards, patient engagement, automated recall, unscheduled-treatment reminders, online scheduling, Patient Finder, payments, forms, and other practice performance workflows.
- Follow Ups specifically covers hygiene recare, unscheduled treatment, past-due balances, and other follow-up tasks.
- Team Tasks makes manual work and ownership visible.

Sources reviewed:

- https://www.dentalintel.com/follow-ups
- https://www.dentalintel.com/scheduling
- https://educate.dentalintel.com/en/articles/10770834-team-tasks
- https://www.dentalintel.com/blog-posts/optimizing-follow-ups-for-unscheduled-treatments-and-hygiene-recare

Editorial implication:

The older framing “Dental Intelligence = analytics, Praxora = next action” is too simplistic and was not used. The page compares a broad all-in-one practice performance platform with a narrower follow-up recovery layer.

## Public-page guardrails

- No claim that Praxora is universally better.
- No fake feature table.
- No fake pricing comparison.
- No claim that a competitor lacks a feature unless supported and essential.
- No fake integration claims.
- No fake case studies or customer results.
- Review-derived pain patterns are used as neutral software-evaluation questions rather than attacks.
